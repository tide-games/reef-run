Provably fair — how the tide decides

In Tide mode your run names a Bitcoin (testnet4) block that does not exist yet. When it is mined — and one more block confirms it — the reefs are placed by sha256(blockHash | mark), your mark being a random nonce fixed when you committed your course. Nobody, not even this page, knows where the reefs lie before the tide comes in, and anyone can recompute them afterwards from public chain data — the verify ✓ in the log does exactly that, in your browser. If a deep reorg ever moves a settled block, verify says so instead of pretending.

You shape your own odds: more reefs and longer courses both raise the payout, and every combination carries the identical 3% house edge — the maths is open in reef.js, pure and testable.

Practice mode is seeded locally and marked as such — instant, but not provable.
