Provably fair — how the tide decides

The tide is Blake's testnet4 — the BLAKE2b hardfork of Bitcoin, which parted from plain testnet4 at block 150,308 — followed by mempool.guide. Runs committed before Reef Run moved to Blake settle and verify against plain testnet4, the chain they were committed to.

In Tide mode your run names a Blake testnet4 block that does not exist yet. When it is mined — and one more block confirms it — the reefs are placed by sha256(blockHash | mark), your mark being a random nonce fixed when you committed your course. Nobody, not even this page, knows where the reefs lie before the tide comes in, and anyone can recompute them afterwards from public chain data — the verify ✓ in the log does exactly that, in your browser. If a deep reorg ever moves a settled block, verify says so instead of pretending.

You shape your own odds: more reefs and longer courses both raise the payout, and every combination carries the identical 3% house edge — the maths is open in reef.js, pure and testable.

Practice mode is seeded locally and marked as such — instant, but not provable.

Doubloons are play money. Part of the fleet · source on GitHub · house edge 3%, identical for every course.
